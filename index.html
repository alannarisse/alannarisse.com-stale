Web Developer for Qadium

Qadium Inc. was a cybersecurity and internet-intelligence startup founded in 2012 by former CIA analyst Tim Junio and Matt Kraning. Qadium is now Expanse.co.

Qadium's tech challenge was that their marketing website was connected to their product lifecycles — being dependent on the applications sprint cycles, the marketing team was unable to release timely materials with quick turn-around. The app development team was also swamped and unable to meet their requests. I was brought on as additional support for marketing content while I researched tech stacks and presented to the C-suite my findings on platforms that would better suit their needs. Researched using a headless Wordpress CMS with React front-end. Settled on a clean, custom Wordpress template that would allow a much sorter turn-around of materials. Created timelines and using their design system, created an on-brand design. Provided qadium with in-depth development and release processes so they could continue to support the site independently from the app dev team.
